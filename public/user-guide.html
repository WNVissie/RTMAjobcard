Getting started

Print a job card or a logistics service note for any RTMA case, filled in from live case data.

  1. Open the app from the CoolerCo SMART Hub (tile RTMA Job Cards) - you are signed in automatically. Or go to rtmajobcard.netlify.app and sign in with your RTMA email and password (the same login as the RTMA dashboard).
  2. Choose a tab: Job Card for one case, or Bulk Job Cards for many.
  3. Print, or choose Save as PDF in the print window.
Nothing is saved in the app. Edits, photos and signatures are lost when you close or reload the page, so print or save the PDF before you leave.

One job card

Search a case, check the details, add anything missing, print.

  1. Type the case number in Search Case (for example CAS-0000123-DEMO) and press Search or Enter.
  2. The card fills in: store, address, dates, technician, asset, the asset's earlier repair cases and this case's repairs. The preview on the right shows the card exactly as it will print.
  3. The type is picked for you from the case type:
    Repair Job CardLogistics Service Note
    Repairs get a job card; deliveries, removals, replacements and warehouse cases get a logistics service note. Click the other button to switch.
  4. Change any field on the left if it is wrong or empty. Use + Add row to add a repair or a symptom.
  5. Optional: add photos of the asset, and a signature - draw it in the box and click Use drawn signature, or upload a picture of a signed page.
  6. Click Print / Save as PDF. Cards print on A4, portrait.
The print button turns green once you have printed, so you can see which card is done.

Asset Repair History
The asset's earlier repair cases in the last 6 months, one line per case, with the outcome. Deliveries, removals and this case itself are not listed.
Repairs Performed (This Case)
The repair lines recorded on this case only, from the repairs upload. If it says none were found, the repairs for this case have not been uploaded yet - add them by hand if you know them.
Symptoms
Blank lines for the technician to fill in.
Faults Found
Not shown yet - still being developed.
Logistics Service Note
Store, dates and driver, with the asset delivered and the asset removed (barcode, model, condition), and a signature page.

Bulk job cards

Print many cases at once - one card per page.

  1. Open Bulk Job Cards and paste the case numbers - one per line, or separated by commas or spaces. Up to 200 at a time.

    Example

  2. Click Load cases. The cases are listed with store, warehouse, status and technician. Case numbers that could not be found are listed in red - check them for typos.
  3. Untick any case you don't want (or use Select all).
  4. Click Print Job Cards or Print Logistics Notes. A new window opens with one page per case; it prints when ready.
  5. Or click Job Cards (ZIP) or Logistics Notes (ZIP) to save one PDF per case instead, each named and numbered with its case number (for example CAS-12345.pdf), all in one ZIP file.
Allow pop-ups for the site if nothing opens. Bulk cards can't be edited - for a card that needs changes, use the Job Card tab.

Questions

"Case not found"
Check the case number. Cases appear once they have been uploaded to the RTMA dashboard (usually daily), so a very new case may not be there yet.
No repairs on the card
The repair lines for that case have not been uploaded yet. You can add them by hand before printing.
No repair history
The asset had no other repair cases in the last 6 months, or the case has no asset recorded.
The login screen keeps coming back
Your session expired - sign in again. Opening the app from the SMART Hub signs you in automatically.
Can I save the card in the app?
No. Use Save as PDF in the print window and keep the file.
